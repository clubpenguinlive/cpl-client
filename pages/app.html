Add Club Penguin Live to your phone

Install it like an app for a full-screen, landscape experience, no browser bars.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open clubpenguinlive.net in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then launch the penguin icon from your home screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open clubpenguinlive.net in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when the banner appears, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. Launch it from your home screen, it opens full-screen.

Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open clubpenguinlive.net.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar (a monitor with a down arrow), or just bookmark it.
